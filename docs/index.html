Developer & Tester Guide

How to Install & Test VeloMetric on Your iPhone

1

Direct Xcode Installation (Instant & Free)

Open VeloMetric.xcodeproj in Xcode on your Mac. Connect your iPhone via USB, select your personal Apple Developer Team under Signing & Capabilities, and press ⌘R to compile and run directly on your device in ~5 seconds.

2

Trust Developer Certificate

On first install, go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management on your iPhone and tap Trust on your Apple ID developer profile.

3

TestFlight Beta Distribution

TestFlight public beta builds are uploaded via App Store Connect. Once an active build is released, tap below to join the public TestFlight beta group.
